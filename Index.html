The Origin of Bluebird Wellness

My grandmother started a cherished family tradition of collecting "Bluebirds of Happiness." To me, bluebirds represent hope, resilience, and joy—the exact foundation I aim to build with everyone who enters my practice.

I am a mom to a 28-year-old, a dedicated clinician with 30 years of experience, and an avid outdoors enthusiast who loves skiing, Michigan sports, reading, hiking, and yoga.
